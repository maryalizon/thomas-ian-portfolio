Upload your media files into this folder.

Suggested filenames:
- portrait.webp
- showreel.mp4
- project-01.mp4
- project-02.mp4

Then open src/App.jsx and use:

const PORTRAIT_URL = "/media/portrait.webp";
const SHOWREEL_URL = "/media/showreel.mp4";

For a portfolio item:
embedUrl: "/media/project-01.mp4",

For faster loading, full-length videos should be hosted on Vimeo, YouTube,
or Cloudinary. Paste their EMBED URL into SHOWREEL_URL or embedUrl.
